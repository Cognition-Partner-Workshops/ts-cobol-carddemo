## Limits and failures

* **Baseline SHA shown: FAIL on all 32 mftb pages.** The plugin prints its version and generation time, not the commit. Not fixed: the pages are scanner output and are not hand-edited. The SHA is in `modernization/INTAKE.md`, both pages written in this step and `fan-out.md`.
* **Counts equal data** on the mftb module, chain and transaction pages was not checked figure by figure; `estate.html` was (see its row).
* In-page links on the mftb pages are routes of the form `#p-<panel>?<filter>` handled by the page's script, not element ids. The script clicked up to ten per page and confirmed the address changed without an error; it did not confirm that each route showed the intended filtered rows.
* On the mftb pages a text filter hides rows and collapses the groups in its panel; after clearing it the groups stay collapsed until reopened. Recorded as page behaviour, not a failure.
* Buttons on the mftb pages include the panel tabs. Clicking a panel tab hides the buttons of the previous panel, so some buttons were not reached in the single pass; none raised an error.
* Collapsibles nested inside table rows that were not expanded were not reached.
* `store/reports/CARDDEMO/CARDDEMO-report.html`: **Links FAIL, not fixed.** 14 links point to transaction pages that were never generated: online/online-CAUP.html, online/online-CAVW.html, online/online-CB00.html, online/online-CCDL.html, online/online-CCLI.html, online/online-CCUP.html, online/online-CR00.html, online/online-CT00.html, online/online-CT01.html, online/online-CT02.html, online/online-CU00.html, online/online-CU01.html, online/online-CU02.html, online/online-CU03.html. The CARDDEMO child wrote 4 of the 18 transaction pages its report links to. Generating the other 14 is that child's work, so the parent did not do it.
